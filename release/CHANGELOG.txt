ELDEN RING: ASCENSION - changelog

{VERSION} - first public release
- 261 cards, 9 Paths, 31 Signature cards, 18 Keystones, 10 Prismatic cards.
- Balance: bonuses stack with soft caps (damage counts fully up to +60%,
  damage taken down to -35%, half beyond), sensible ceilings for lifesteal,
  attack speed, allies and status buildup.
- Title screen shows the mod name and version.
- Works with a plain me3 launch on Windows and Linux: the mod finds your
  save folder by itself.
