----- DESCRIPTION -----
ELDEN RING: ASCENSION - Roguelite cards for the Lands Between, by Lord Bink.

Every boss is a card draw. Beat a boss for the first time and choose 1 of 3 cards. Each card is a permanent power for that character. It can add damage, cast a spell when you perfect dodge or land a critical, build up statuses, strengthen your allies, earn you more runes or keep you alive. Pick the same card again and it grows a level, up to V. Cards range from Common all the way up to Mythic, and above Mythic sit the ultra-rare Prismatic cards. No two characters end up the same.

This is the first public release, and so far it has only been played on Lord Bink's own Linux (Proton) setup. If anything seems wrong, please report it in the Bugs tab and attach logs\runtime.jsonl from the mod folder.

AI disclosure: this mod was built with AI. The code, the card designs, the art scripts and the sound scripts were written by AI coding agents, Claude (Anthropic, in Claude Code) and Codex (OpenAI), and the whole mod was directed and played by Lord Bink. The card artwork is painted procedurally and the sounds are synthesized by the mod's own scripts, so no game assets are reused.

ELDEN RING belongs to FromSoftware / Bandai Namco. This is a fan-made mod and is not affiliated with them.

----- INSTALLATION INSTRUCTIONS -----
Windows
1. Install Mod Engine 3 (me3): https://github.com/garyttierney/me3/releases
2. Extract the zip to a folder you can write to, for example Documents\Ascension. Do not use Program Files or the game folder.
3. Double-click "Launch Ascension.bat" (or ascension.me3).
4. When the mod is running, the title screen shows ASCENSION and the version in the bottom-left corner.

Linux / Steam Deck
1. Install Mod Engine 3 for Linux. ELDEN RING must use Proton, and Steam must be running.
2. Extract the zip to a folder in your home directory.
3. Run launch-ascension-linux.sh.

First steps
The mod uses its own save file (ER0000.ascension.sl2), so your normal save is never touched. Start a new character and play offline. Press F7 to open the collection. Its Guide tab explains everything in short lines.

Updating
When you install a new version, keep the "state" and "local" folders, because your cards are stored there.

Uninstalling
Delete the mod folder. Your normal save was never changed. The Ascension save stays in the game's save folder until you delete it yourself.

----- MAIN FEATURES -----
- 261 cards with 5 levels each, from Common to Mythic plus Prismatic. Every card has its own artwork.
- 9 Paths: Ferocity, Celerity, Malice, Resonance, Fortitude, Evasion, Thirst, Fortune and Command. Every card level gives a Path point, and 5, 10 and 15 points unlock a Path bonus.
- 31 Signature cards: the first time you beat a great boss from the base game or Shadow of the Erdtree, it always offers its own card with its own spell, such as Loretta's greatbow, Rennala's full moon or the Starscourge's meteorite.
- 18 Keystones: legendary cards that change how you play.
- 10 Prismatic cards: the rarest tier, above Mythic. About two show up in a whole playthrough. They shine in every colour at once and turn over with a very loud MAX WIN. At full level they give things like +30 to every attribute, four Meteorites of Astel every 3 seconds, or surviving a deadly blow every 45 seconds.
- Every attribute gets a second role on top of what it already does. Vigor helps you survive, Mind adds FP and spirit power, Endurance adds stamina and poise, Strength boosts heavy and charged attacks, Dexterity speeds up attacks and ripostes, Intelligence powers dodges and card spells, Faith adds protection and better flasks, and Arcane brings runes, luck and statuses. The panel next to Level Up shows what each point adds before you confirm. Talismans and Physick tears that raise attributes raise these bonuses too.
- Embers: spend them to reroll an offer, lock a card for the next offer, banish a card for good, or take runes instead of a card.
- A harder world: enemies and bosses are stronger and give more runes. The Tide rises as your cards grow, and from Tide I bosses can come back as Ascended bosses with extra affixes and a better reward.
- Combat moments: STANCE BROKEN, RIPOSTE, BACKSTAB, PERFECT DODGE, EXECUTED and RAMPAGE appear on screen with their own sounds, together with the names of the cards that reacted.
- Balanced with care: bonuses stack with soft caps, so strong builds feel strong without breaking the game.
- All in-game text is in English, short and with simple numbers.

----- REQUIREMENTS -----
- ELDEN RING on Steam, game version {APP_VERSION} exactly (the title screen shows "App Ver. {APP_VERSION}"), on Windows or on Linux / Steam Deck with Proton. After a game update, wait for a mod update.
- Mod Engine 3 (me3): https://github.com/garyttierney/me3/releases
- The game must have been started at least once, so that its save folder exists.
- Shadow of the Erdtree is not required, but the mod fully covers it: all 42 DLC bosses give cards, and 11 of the 31 Signature cards come from DLC bosses, including Messmer, Rellana, Bayle and Promised Consort Radahn. Without the DLC you get everything from the base game. The mod was made and played with the DLC; setups without it have not been tried yet.

Compatibility
- It does not work together with other mods that replace regulation.bin (Convergence, Reforged, randomizers and similar) or with Seamless Co-op.
- It is offline only.
- If an old dinput8.dll or winhttp.dll mod loader is in the game folder, move it out, otherwise the mod switches itself off.
